# Explore and pose the fused manikin

Open [the interactive manikin](http://127.0.0.1:8766/viewers/mannequin-articulation/) through the local server. It loads the complete prepared Gaussian scan, with both captures and all 16 body regions. The original scan files stay unchanged. The page shares the homepage's typography, colors, and automatic light/dark theme.

The published site loads the four prepared scan binaries in `viewers/mannequin-fusion/` and the accepted `joint-annotations.json` and `joint-refinement.json` bundled beside this page. On localhost, a saved map in `raw/mannequin-fused/` takes priority; without one, the bundled map is used. Keep the two bundled JSON files as exact copies of the accepted local files when publishing updated joints, because the geometry refinement checks the annotation file's hash. Original PLY files, intermediate scans, and local projection images are not published. Pose export/import remains available on the static site; saving a joint map into the project requires the local server.

1. Compare **Fused**, **Front scan**, and **Back scan**, or choose **Segmentation** to see the colored body regions and their legend. Segmentation shows both captures with the same color for each region; it does not change the Gaussian shapes or opacity. Front/back selects a capture, not a camera direction.
2. Choose **Scan pose**, **Lie flat**, or **Upper body up**. **Lie flat** places the manikin face up with the arms and legs alongside the body. **Upper body up** raises the torso on a 45° backrest while both legs stay extended along the full mattress, respecting the existing hip limits and fixed waist. The mattress, frame, and backrest are simple generated reference geometry, not another Gaussian scan.
3. Select a joint in **Joint rotation**, then move its sliders. Ball joints have three controls; hinges and arm swivels have one. **Play joint demo** cycles through the selected joint's rotation directions within its limits and shows its center and axes. **Stop demo** restores the pose from before the demonstration; it does not leave the preview motion in your pose.
4. **Reset** returns the whole manikin to **Scan pose** and removes the reference bed. Open **Pro** when you need the detailed controls described below.

Drag anywhere in the scan to orbit the camera, right-drag or Shift-drag to pan, and scroll to zoom. Use the joint sliders, preset buttons, or joint demo to pose the manikin. The preset buttons also frame their poses; use **Pro → Full body** to fit the current scene again.

**Pro** reveals angle entry in degrees and travel limits, exact camera directions, **Focus joint**, **Isolate branch**, **All joints**, and **Rotation axes**. It also contains motion response, geometry checks, joint-map editing, and pose import/export. With joint markers visible, click a marker to select its joint, then use the angle controls.

Under **Pro → Motion response**, enable **Spring motion** for a gradual response. Higher **Stiffness** pulls more strongly toward the target; higher **Damping** reduces oscillation. **Nudge joint** applies a small rotational impulse. These response settings apply to the angle controls. Turn spring motion off for immediate posing. **Reset this joint** requests zero angles within the selected joint's current limits.

The forearm, wrist pad, and hand on each side move together as one rigid body. Arm swivels rotate around the arm's length. There is no separate wrist joint. A parent joint carries its connected branch, so shoulder movement carries the arm and hip movement carries the leg. Both scan captures receive the same body transform, including rotation of each Gaussian's shape.

Spring settings use a normalized rotational response. Scan units have no calibrated physical scale, and stiffness/damping are not measured mechanical properties. There are no collision, gravity, or contact constraints. Body parts can pass through one another at unsuitable angles; segmented surfaces may reveal seams when moved away from their scanned pose. The trousers remain part of the static segmented scan and cannot deform naturally through a large hip bend, so the raised-back pose can expose garment seams or overlap the reference mattress.

Current poses, their bed presentation, and resistance settings are kept in this browser when storage is available. In **Pro**, use **Export pose** for a separate JSON copy and **Import pose** to restore it. Pose files include `presentationPreset` (`scan`, `lying`, or `sitting`) and `presentationRevision: 2`, so importing a bed pose also restores its whole-body placement and reference bed. The internal `sitting` ID now means **Upper body up**. An explicit import of an earlier `sitting` layout is rejected rather than restoring the old dangling-leg pose on the revised bed; choose **Upper body up** to start from the corrected preset. Older pose files without a presentation preset open in the scan presentation. Keep the corresponding saved joint map with an exported pose. Imports check the scan identity and the joint centers, axes, connections, types, and limits before changing the current pose.

Browser drafts use `mannequin-fused-pose-v3:<rig signature>`. If no `v3` draft exists, the page can load the corresponding `v2` draft without overwriting it. An earlier `sitting` draft is migrated to the revised **Upper body up** preset; other compatible drafts retain their saved joint angles. Earlier `v1` drafts remain untouched and are not automatically loaded. While a joint demo is playing, draft saves retain the pose from before the demo.

To keep the fitted centers while fine-tuning, open **Pro → Joint center & geometry check** and choose **Export current joint map**. Follow **Edit joint map**, import that file, inspect and adjust the joints from multiple views, then choose **Save for articulation** and reload this page. The export also includes your current stiffness and damping settings. The map is saved locally at `raw/mannequin-fused/joint-annotations.json`; a subsequent save keeps the previous file as `joint-annotations.previous.json`.

The optional geometry check is a derived file at `raw/mannequin-fused/joint-refinement.json`. Its refined positions are used only when its input hash matches the exact saved annotation file and its scan identity matches the current fusion. It does not overwrite the saved annotations or the scan. Changing and saving the joint map invalidates the old refinement; after reloading, the page uses the newly saved map until a matching geometry check is produced. Changes to joint geometry or the scan also invalidate incompatible saved poses. Label and note changes alone do not change a pose's geometric signature.

Expand **Joint center & geometry check** to see the active center, axis, attached branch, and refinement note. Visible pads, clothing, and body shells cannot establish every hidden mechanical center, so inspect the resulting movement before treating an estimated joint as final.

The current audit refines the neck center with opposing surface sections, and knee/ankle centers and axes with paired visible metal hinge caps. The measured arm collars, shoulders, hips and fixed waist retain their accepted positions. Positive knee angles bend posteriorly. **View projection check** opens the saved multi-angle evidence for that joint. Reproduce the audit with `.venv-fusion/bin/python tools/analyze_joint_geometry.py`; it reads the saved annotations and fused scan and writes only a derived report and review images.

If the local server is stopped, run `python3 tools/serve.py 8766` from the repository. Everything runs locally; no scan or annotation is uploaded.
