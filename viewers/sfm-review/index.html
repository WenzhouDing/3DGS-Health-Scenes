How this scene reads

Mostly green-to-yellow and red-to-gold, so coverage is healthy throughout. Two spots worth knowing:

the blue patch at the south end of the ceiling — ~10–30 views instead of ~150. Still fine, but artifacts will show there first.

Gaps in the grey cloud are not coverage holes. A fifth of the ceiling has no reconstructed points because blank panelling gives nothing to match — it was filmed, it just has no texture. Coverage is measured on the grid, never on the cloud.

Scale is arbitrary. Floor to ceiling is 6.55 scene units, not metres.
