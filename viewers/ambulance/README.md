# Ambulance with articulated manikin

Open `http://127.0.0.1:8766/viewers/ambulance/` using the project's local server (`python3 tools/serve.py 8766`). The page places the complete fused manikin on the scanned stretcher. Both captures and all 16 regions use the same fitted articulation map as the standalone manikin viewer. The ambulance SOG and source scan binaries are unchanged by this integration.

The manikin is fitted at scale 0.75, about 10% smaller than the initial ambulance placement, and rests on the mattress without blocking surface overlaps.

**Pose manikin** shows handles at the articulated joints. Select and drag a handle horizontally, or drag its rotation ring, to adjust the selected axis. Use the X/Y/Z (or Bend/Twist) buttons to choose the axis. Arrow keys on a focused handle adjust by 2°; Shift increases this to 10°. **Precise angles** exposes sliders and numeric controls. Drag the background to orbit, right-drag to pan, and scroll to zoom. **Focus joint** moves closer to the selected joint.

**Arms fall on release** is enabled by default. Holding a shoulder or arm-swivel handle supports that whole arm while you pose it. Releasing the pointer lets gravity move the shoulder and swivel; grabbing it again catches it. The precise sliders and arrow keys use the same hold/release behavior. Uncheck the option to keep manually posed arms in place. Torso placement, neck and legs stay posed.

Arm motion uses gravitational torque from the captured arm mass distribution, damping, joint limits and swept contact checks. It does not spring back to a saved pose. Contact checks include the mattress/cabin and approximate fitted central torso, pelvis, head and opposite-arm volumes, with attachment regions and loose lateral clothing excluded. This is a simplified articulated simulation, without tissue deformation or a calibrated physical mass/length scale.

**Undo/Redo** groups each drag into one adjustment; Cmd/Ctrl+Z and Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Z also work outside text inputs. Escape cancels the current drag. **Reset joint** restores the selected joint's fitted angle, and **Reset onto bed** restores the entire fitted pose and placement. **Prevent surface collisions** is on initially: requested moves are checked at intermediate joint angles and stop at the last clear pose.

**Explore cabin** hides joint handles and enables camera movement with WASD and look dragging. Camera collisions apply in this fly mode; Pose mode uses an orbit inspection camera. Bed and cabin view buttons return to useful inspection positions. **Scene & placement** contains small placement offsets, manikin visibility, and an overlay of the collision surfaces.

**Whole-body drop test** provides a separate controlled drop with the current joint pose held during that test. **Lift & drop** sweeps the body upward by the chosen scene-unit distance, then releases it; **Drop from here** releases the current placement. Playback defaults to quarter speed so contact is visible. Pause/Resume preserves the fall, Cancel or Escape restores the starting placement, and Undo reverses the completed drop as one edit. Pose editing is locked during a drop; camera inspection remains available. Leaving the tab or window pauses the test until Resume is pressed. Arm gravity also pauses when leaving the window; grabbing and releasing an arm resumes it.

Gravity uses 9.81 scene units/s² with fixed 1/120-second steps, swept translations no larger than 0.004 units, and refined contact stopping against the same captured body samples and surface proxies. Slow frames are capped rather than caught up, and gravity always enforces scene contacts. This tests vertical translation only: joints and body orientation remain fixed, with no bounce, friction, tipping, ragdoll dynamics or mattress deformation. Scene units have not been calibrated to meters, so fall timing is illustrative.

Collision geometry is stored separately from appearance. `bed-placement.json` records the stretcher fit and bounded surfaces for the floor, ceiling, walls, stretcher and furniture. `scene-collision.glb` is generated from these same proxies for the camera. It does not use the old `index.voxel` collider or make residual Gaussian haze solid. Regenerate it from the repository root with `python3 tools/ambulance/build_contact_mesh.py`.

The body checker samples compact, opaque Gaussians from both captures, carries the samples through the articulated hierarchy, and tests against the scene proxies. It is approximate sampled contact checking, not a calibrated physical simulation. Scale is fitted to the stretcher; deformable mattress/clothing and general whole-body self-collision are not simulated. Fine equipment and objects absent from the proxy model are not checked. Original joint travel limits remain enforced even when surface prevention is off; released arms always use contact checks.

The standalone ambulance without the manikin remains available with `?nomanikin`. `?webgl` selects the WebGL renderer. Automatic camera animation is off by default; add `?animate` to enable it. The static viewer uses the committed prepared captures and joint map; original raw captures and intermediate audits stay outside the published file set.
